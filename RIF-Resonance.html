Quick start

  1. Download or open the app for your device. Every public version is supplied without scripts.
  2. Save the private .riflibrary attachment that is sent to you individually by email.
  3. In the app, tap Choose library from Files and select that emailed .riflibrary file.
  4. For a mobile Home Screen icon, open the app in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.

Audio wellness tool only. RIF Resonance is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent disease. Start at low volume and use your device’s normal audio output.
